I am T.M. Sajith Priyankara, a Ph.D. candidate in Statistics and a Graduate Part-Time Instructor at Texas Tech University. 

My research focuses on functional data analysis, statistics on manifolds, multivariate analysis, and object data analysis. I am particularly interested in real-world applications in bioinformatics and public health.

## Highlights

[FUSED: A Functional Representation for Joint Structural and Elemental Analysis of Protein Ligand Binding Sites](/publication/fused-functional-representation) has been **accepted** for publication in the *Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling*. FUSED jointly captures the spatial structure and elemental composition of protein–ligand binding sites through functional representations, supporting binding-site classification and pocket matching. [Read the bioRxiv preprint](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.07.10.737806).
